Nexon EV at a glance

The Nexon EV is the model that put mass-market EVs on the map in India, pairing a spacious SUV body with a choice of two battery packs. The 45 kWh cars cover a claimed 465 km; the 30 kWh Creative Plus MR trades 140 km of that for ₹2.5 lakh off the price.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is the claimed range?

465 km on the 45 kWh packs fitted to Fearless 45 and Empowered+ A 45, and 325 km on the 30 kWh Creative Plus MR. Both are ARAI test figures — expect roughly 70 to 80 per cent of that in mixed real-world driving.

How long does it take to charge?

On a DC fast charger, 10 to 80 per cent takes 56 minutes. On a home AC charger, empty to full takes about eight and a half hours, which is an overnight charge. The port is CCS2, the standard on Indian public networks.

Which variant offers the best value?

Fearless 45. It carries the same 45 kWh pack and the same 465 km claimed range as the Empowered+ A 45, for two lakh less. The Creative Plus MR saves a further ₹2.5 lakh but gives up 140 km of range, which is the difference between charging weekly and charging every few days.
